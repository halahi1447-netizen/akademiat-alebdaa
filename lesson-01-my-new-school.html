Reading

📖 My New School

Ali is a new student.

Today is his first day at school.

He walks to his classroom with his blue backpack.

His teacher smiles and says, "Welcome, Ali!"

Ali feels a little nervous.

Soon, he meets a friendly boy named Omar.

Omar says, "Let's sit together."

They read a storybook.

They write with their pencils.

At break time, they play football.

Everyone is kind to Ali.

Ali smiles. He says, "I love my new school."
